Combine and discover 300 different elements and complete the quests. Put two elements in the cauldron and find out whether the world has a name for what they make.
## Controls
- <kbd>↑</kbd> <kbd>←</kbd> <kbd>↓</kbd> <kbd>→</kbd> or <kbd>W</kbd> <kbd>A</kbd> <kbd>S</kbd> <kbd>D</kbd> : Move selection
- <kbd>Enter</kbd>, <kbd>Space</kbd>, finger tap, mouse click: Pick, lock selection or clear the selected element
- <kbd>H</kbd>: Hint (for when you're stuck, costs a move)
- <kbd>M</kbd>: Toggle sound

Also supports gamepad:  d-pad or left stick to move selection, Ⓐ to pick and mix, Ⓨ hint, Ⓑ let go, Ⓧ mute, Start for the menu.
